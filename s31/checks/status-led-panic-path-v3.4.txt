status_led_halt() each wait on the cycle counter (rdcycle, sub, bltu back to the rdcycle) and
the instruction after it.  The store to GPIO+0x14 (out1_w1ts) raises GPIO37, the one to
GPIO+0x18 (out1_w1tc) lowers it.
